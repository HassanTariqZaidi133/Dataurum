About

Built around one idea.

Data has value. We help businesses realize it.

Dataurum is a technology company focused on helping organizations make better use of their data. Through analytics, business intelligence, ERP systems, custom software, automation, and AI, we build the digital infrastructure that turns information into measurable business outcomes.

The name combines Data with Aurum — the Latin word for gold. It reflects our core philosophy: data, treated with the right precision and intelligence, is one of the most valuable assets a business can have.
